Settings

This keeps the connection busy all the time, so it uses data at your full speed: roughly 2 GB a minute at 300 Mbps. The auto-stop protects capped plans.

Step length matters for map accuracy: walk 10 normal steps, measure the distance, and divide by 10 (roughly 0.41 × your height). The map works by counting steps and tracking which way the phone points. It can't see walls, so expect drift of around 5–10% of the distance walked; walk back to the start to see how far it has drifted.

In Both mode, download and upload run at the same time and share the radio link, so each can read a little lower than when tested alone.
